Kipster tools have plain, verb-first names: `post_chat_message`, `transcribe_voice_note`, `copy_attachment_to_workspace`, `ask_user_question`, `request_user_approval`, `list_kips`, `get_kip`, `delegate_to_kip`, `check_kip_task`, `create_rich_doc`, `list_rich_docs`, `read_rich_doc`, `edit_rich_doc`, `delete_rich_doc`, `search_memory`, `list_recent_threads`, `read_thread`, `save_memory`, `forget_memory`, `manage_data_tables`, `manage_vector_collections`, `list_admin_operations` and `run_admin_operation`. The old names are removed. Adapters record a provider's own questions and approvals under the new names.
