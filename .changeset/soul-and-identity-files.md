Each kip has two identity files: `soul.md`, its character and working rules, and `identity.md`, its role. `AGENTS.md` is removed; the starter kips' working rules move into a "How I work" section of their `soul.md`, and the identity routes, events and administration operations accept only the two files. Protocol 2 drops `AGENTS.md` from identity file names; Core still serves protocol 1 clients.
